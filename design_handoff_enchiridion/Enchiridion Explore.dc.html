5c Sorting, and what it costs

Default order is curated, not alphabetical: texts first, then supplements and references, then modules — era, then year within each. Sorting a column replaces that judgement with a mechanical one, so the footer always names the order you're looking at. Clicking a third time returns to curated.

YEAR ↑  — OLDEST FIRST, BCE NEGATIVE
TITLE ↑  — IGNORES A LEADING "THE"
TYPE ↑  — TEXT · SUPPLEMENT · REFERENCE · MODULE
ERA ↑  — CHRONOLOGICAL, NOT ALPHABETICAL
MODULES SORT LAST UNDER ERA — THEY HAVE NONE
