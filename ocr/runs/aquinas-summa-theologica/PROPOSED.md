# Proposed transcription

Adopt `aquinas-summa-theologica.md` as the transcription produced by this run.

It contains Aquinas's complete authored, unfinished *Summa*: 512 questions and
2,669 articles, ending where Aquinas stopped at III, question 90.  The
posthumous *Supplementum* is deliberately excluded because another hand
compiled it from Aquinas's earlier *Commentary on the Sentences*; it is not
authorial matter written for this work.

The output was rebuilt byte-identically, passed the diagnostic triad, and was
checked for structural sequence, Gutenberg boilerplate, editorial apparatus,
raw HTML/LaTeX, anchors, fences, entities, and foreign-script artifacts.  Adopt
at `needs-review`: no independent witness was consulted, because the supplied
PDFs and EPUBs are renderings of the same Gutenberg transcription and their
agreement establishes fidelity rather than correctness.
