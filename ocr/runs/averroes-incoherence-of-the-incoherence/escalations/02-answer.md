# Follow-up: repair the subset your own document establishes

Your work queue is exactly the right artifact and it stays. But the rule you
applied to it was misapplied — my fault in how I stated it — and part of that
queue should be repaired now rather than deferred.

**"Never repair a variant you have not seen printed" is a stage-4 rule.** It
governs adjudication against an external witness. Stage 3 repairs on **internal
evidence**, and needs no printed page. `ocr/3-postprocess/STAGE.md` has been
updated with the distinction; read the new section before you begin.

The test is **where the evidence lives**, plus **exactly one repair available**.
