Two things specific to this text. Price's covering letter and his appendix are part of what was published — the essay reached the Royal Society through Price after Bayes's death and has never circulated without him — so treat them as the work rather than as editorial apparatus, and attribute clearly in the text so a reader is never uncertain whose voice they are in.
