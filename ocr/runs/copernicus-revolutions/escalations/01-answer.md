Two things to be careful about from here.

**The 140 images are the argument, not decoration.** This book's diagrams carry
its geometry, and an image attached at the wrong point is worse than one
missing, because it reads as authoritative. Verify that every reference resolves
and that figures sit where the text calls for them. Say plainly in the record

astronomical tables, and OCR errors inside them are invisible to every check we
have — a wrong digit is well-formed. Do not repair any figure in a table on
internal evidence; a number has no context that forces it. List doubtful ones by
printed page for the reviewer instead.
