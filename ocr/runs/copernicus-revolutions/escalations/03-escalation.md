# ESCALATION: unlisted critical-apparatus label forms

The authorized forms are present and can be handled exactly as directed:
`[Earlier draft:]`, `[Printed text:]`, `[Deleted version:]`, and `[In the
autograph ...]`. The OCR also contains the following label forms not on that
list. Per the count-guard instruction, I have stopped rather than treating them
as synonyms.

## Variant-label synonyms

- `[Earlier version:]` — printed pp. 272, 303, 310, 312, and 321.
- `[Earlier version of the beginning of V, 1:]` — p. 226.
- `[Earlier version of the concluding paragraph of V, 23:]` — p. 276.
- `[Printed version:]` — pp. 226, 277, 305, 312, and 321.
- `[Deleted in the autograph:]` — p. 227.

Proposed treatment, if authorized: treat `Earlier version` like `Earlier
draft` (remove label and variant), `Printed version` like `Printed text`
(remove label but retain the received passage), and `Deleted in the autograph`
like `Deleted version` (remove label and rejected passage).

## Prose labels introducing variants

- `[Here Copernicus originally planned to include ... which he later deleted
  ... reads as follows].` — p. 25.
- `[The foregoing letter ... ended Book I as originally planned ... material,
  which was subsequently deleted ... reads as follows].` — p. 26.
- `[An earlier version of the latter part of II, 12 survives in the autograph
  ...]` — p. 78.
- `[The beginning of a new book, according to Copernicus' original plan; an
  earlier draft ... is translated here].` — p. 80.

Proposed treatment, if authorized: remove each prose label together with the
rejected/alternate passage it introduces, stopping at the next explicit
received-text label or validated chapter boundary.

## Standalone editorial notices

- `[The rest of the material deleted here in the autograph was printed ... as
  the following beginning of I, 12].` — p. 26.
- `[As the heading of I, 12, the first edition introduced ...]` — p. 26.
- `[Book II, Chapter N, according to Copernicus' original plan]` — pp. 27, 40,
  and 42.
- `[Marginal note, inserted in the wrong place, and later deleted, but restored
  by the editors: ...]` — p. 154.

Proposed treatment, if authorized: remove each entire notice as editorial
scholarship. The p. 154 notice includes the restored authorial note inside the
same brackets; under the received-work rule, both label and restored/deleted
note would go.

Please confirm whether these proposed mappings are authorized. No apparatus
removal or other Markdown edit has been applied. The raw OCR and root working
copy remain byte-identical, and ordinary sentence-level bracketed
interpolations remain untouched.
