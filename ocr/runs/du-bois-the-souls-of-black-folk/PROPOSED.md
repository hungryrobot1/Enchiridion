# Proposed transcription

Propose `du-bois-the-souls-of-black-folk.md` for adoption at `needs-review`.

It contains the complete 1903 work as reproduced in the supplied Oxford
edition: title, authorial dedication, Forethought, all fourteen chapters, all
nineteen printed musical passages, and After-Thought through “THE END.” The
embedded prose layer was conserved and mechanically checked across all 174
substantive pages; paragraph and verse structure came from the PDF's geometry.
The musical passages were rasterized directly from vector drawing commands and
visually checked for complete crops and sequence. Renderer controls, the
diagnostic triad, duplicate-leaf control/scan, figure-audit controls/audit, and
the text-specific conservation verifier all pass.

This is not a claim of completed proofreading. There is no independent witness,
and the prose has not been read cover to cover against the printed page.
