On the missing appendix — **proceed without it, and record the omission prominently.** Reasoning, so you can disagree on evidence: the 18 pages Crew and de Salvio omit are Galileo's earlier treatise on centres of gravity of solids, appended to the 1638 Discorsi rather than continuous with the Four Days. Splicing another translator's rendering into this one would produce a composite edition that no reader ever held, and this project is deliberate about which edition it publishes. If the appendix is wanted later it belongs as its own entry, which is how Archimedes was handled.

So: note in NOTES.md and in the review record that this edition omits it, name what it contains, and let the syllabus decide. Do not attempt to source or reconstruct it.
