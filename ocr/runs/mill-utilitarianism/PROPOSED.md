I propose `mill-utilitarianism.md` for adoption as the machine-processed,
`needs-review` transcription of John Stuart Mill's complete *Utilitarianism*.

It rebuilds deterministically from the supplied EPUB extraction, retains all
five chapters and four authorial notes, and is byte-for-byte reproducible. Its
27,684 visible retained tokens agree with an independent extraction of the
sibling generated PDF after asserted page-number filtering. Controlled renderer
checks and structural/apparatus scans pass. The EPUB and PDF are two renderings
of one Project Gutenberg transcription, so this verifies extraction fidelity,
not correctness against a printed witness; stage 4 remains for human review.
