# Proposed transcription

Propose `wollstonecraft-vindication-of-the-rights-of-woman.md` for adoption as
`needs-review`.

It contains Wollstonecraft's complete work: her dedication to Talleyrand,
introduction, thirteen chapters in sequence, eleven printed internal section
divisions, and all 22 authorial footnotes. The Project Gutenberg production
credits, edition contents, unsigned biographical sketch, boilerplate, and
licence are excluded.

The result is reproducible with `derive.sh` and `build_wollstonecraft.py`.
The controlled completeness check accounts for every EPUB word as retained,
declared apparatus, or the declared `prettyfoot` to `pretty foot` repair. The
controlled diagnostic triad passes. Since the text has no mathematics, the
triad establishes renderer/debris compatibility rather than verbal correctness.

The EPUB and PDF are two renderings of one Project Gutenberg transcription, so
their agreement establishes fidelity, not correctness against print. No
independent printed witness is supplied; stage 4 is therefore incomplete. The
single bounded doubtful reading is recorded in `NOTES.md`. This proposal does
not change `ocr_status` or claim completed proofreading.
