# Thematic Tracks

Focused paths through the Enchiridion library, each tracing a major intellectual thread across eras. These are substantial undertakings — expect weeks to months per track depending on depth of engagement and supplementary work.

Texts marked with **[NEW]** are not yet in the library and would need to be sourced. Texts marked with **(also in: Track Name)** appear in multiple tracks. Supplements noted in *[brackets]* are planned or proposed.

- **The God of the Philosophers**: Aristotle → Maimonides → Aquinas → Spinoza → Leibniz — what reason can and cannot prove about God
