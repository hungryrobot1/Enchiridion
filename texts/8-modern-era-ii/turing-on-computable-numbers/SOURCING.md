# Rights — On Computable Numbers (1936/37)

Investigated 2026-08-10. **Not settled. This text is currently published and the
US term has not run.** Left flagged deliberately; `metadata.json` has no `rights`
field because nothing here is a clearance.

## What is established

- Published in *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society*, series 2, vol.
  42, pp. 230–265. Cited variously as 1936 and 1937; the printed notice reads
  **`Copyright © London Mathematical Society 1937`**.
- The copyright holder is the **LMS**, not Turing's estate and not a commercial
  publisher. Wiley hosts it under licence from the society.
- Turing died 7 June 1954.

## The two jurisdictions disagree, and the one that binds is the worse one

| | term | status |
|---|---|---|
| **UK** | life + 70 → end of 2024 | **public domain since 1 Jan 2025** |
| **US** | URAA-restored, 95 years from publication | **in copyright until ~2032** |

The US term governs: the site is served from GitHub Pages, in the US. A work
being free in its country of origin does not make it free here — URAA restored
foreign works that were still in copyright at home on 1 January 1996, and this
one was (it had 29 years left to run in the UK at that date).

**Do not be talked out of this by a public-domain notice on a scan.** Internet
Archive applies a blanket "public domain in the USA" statement to volumes it
digitises, and a web search will repeat it back as though it were a finding about
this paper. It is not. The same mistake — treating availability as permission —
is the one the Gödel run cost 148k tokens to learn.

## The recommendation: ask

This is the best permissions prospect in the flagged set. The LMS is a **learned
society**, the request is non-profit educational reproduction of a ninety-year-old
article, and the paper is the society's most celebrated publication rather than a
revenue line. An email costs nothing and the odds are genuinely good — far better
than Springer on Sigler's *Liber Abaci*.

Until an answer arrives the honest options are the standing ones: **point to it
rather than host it** (Wiley has it, and the ETH Zurich copy is openly readable),
keep the curriculum entry, keep supplements and recommended passages. "Read
whole" has never required "hosted by us."

## What would change the answer

A licence grant from the LMS; or evidence that the paper was published in the US
substantially simultaneously (which would defeat URAA restoration and put it on
a renewal analysis instead); or 1 January 2033.
